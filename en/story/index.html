My full story

Why feeling better matters to me.

A different way to look at my health.

In 2019, a serious health challenge pushed me to start looking at my health differently. I began paying closer attention to nutrition, lifestyle, what to eat, what to avoid, and what I could do to better support my health.

That journey eventually became the inspiration behind FeelGr8Again.

A new question worth exploring.

When I came across REDOX, what caught my attention wasn’t another promise of a miracle. It was that the idea was different from anything I had encountered before.

That journey introduced me to redox signaling molecules — and the role they play in cellular communication.

It didn’t give me every answer. It gave me a new question worth exploring.

I didn’t know what my experience would be. I didn’t expect miracles. I simply wanted to see for myself.

You should get to decide for yourself.

I don’t believe anyone should try something simply because I tell them to. Learn about it, ask questions, understand what makes it different, and then decide whether you want to explore it for yourself.

Because for me, this was never about trying to turn back the clock and trying to feel younger. It’s about feeling better — and maybe, somewhere along the way, feeling great again.

When personalized plates became available in Quebec, I chose FEELGR8. That’s how long this idea has been with me.
